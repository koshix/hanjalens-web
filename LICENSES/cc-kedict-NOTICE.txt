Hanja Lens v1.8 does not bundle the full cc-kedict database. At runtime it may fetch
kedict.yml from the upstream repository, extract entries that contain one unambiguous
Hanja spelling, normalize those Hanja to Japanese shinjitai, and cache the resulting
mapping locally in the user's browser.
